Rebecca J. Powell, PhD, is the Director of Data & Research Operations at Fors Marsh. Her research interests focus on visual design of questionnaires and contact materials. Dr. Powell is a Certified RStudio Tidyverse Trainer and has taught courses and webinars on data management in R and tidy survey analysis in R at both AAPOR and MAPOR. She has a PhD in Survey Research and Methodology from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and a BS and MS in Applied Statistics from Rochester Institute of Technology.
